<a id="reuse"></a>
## Reuse

Original prose with valid permission uses **CC BY-SA 4.0**. Copying, redistribution, adaptation and commercial use are allowed. When sharing, provide reasonable attribution, retain supplied copyright, license and source information, link the license, indicate changes and retain previous modification notices. Share adaptations under ShareAlike, following the [full terms](https://creativecommons.org/licenses/by-sa/4.0/legalcode.en) for compatible licenses. Do not add terms or technical measures that restrict licensed rights or imply the original author's endorsement.

Suggested attribution includes the article title, supplied authors or attribution information (a revision-history link may help), source and version links, a CC BY-SA 4.0 link and modification details. The site does not require public GitHub email disclosure for attribution; retain the lawful attribution supplied with the entry.

CC licensing does not transfer copyright. Once validly granted, the license cannot be unilaterally revoked for users complying with its terms. Account closure, withdrawn GitHub attribution consent or stopping article display does not revoke recipients' existing permission. The license does not restrict uses that are lawful without permission.

Check licenses and exclusion notices for the specific material before reuse. Distribute GPL code under its terms, retaining copyright and license notices and providing corresponding source. Game assets and reproduced material do not become CC under the prose license. Obtain permission for portions lacking an applicable license or authorization unless law permits the use.

Attribution, public availability, nonprofit status or an educational-use statement does not itself grant permission or automatically remove liability. Distinguish quotations from your summaries, recording authors, source links, dates and the license or basis of permission. Do not copy whole guides without permission or remove watermarks. Encyclopedia use alone does not authorize relicensing commercial assets.

<a id="contributions"></a>
## Contributions and existing material

Submit only material you have authority to provide, identifying quotations and asset sources. Do not mark third-party material under the project's open license without verification. Current game-image records establish provenance, while redistribution permission remains unverified. Confirm permission, replace or remove them before public distribution.

The project policy for new original prose contributions is CC BY-SA 4.0. Contributors can license only portions they have authority to license. Before opening formal contributions, show the license and explain commercial reuse and irrevocability at submission, record explicit agreement to that policy version and retain exclusions for quotations and assets. **Submission-license confirmation and server-side consent records are not implemented yet; submitting must not be presented as complete permission.**

A new policy must not retroactively cover existing authors' works without authority. Handle existing prose individually through author consent, an existing valid license or replacement. The two formal original articles written for this repository now state CC BY-SA 4.0 in their bodies; the imported V7.3.1 content and assets are not automatically relicensed. GitHub email attribution consent and content licensing are separate choices.

<a id="rights-reports"></a>
## Rights concerns

A copyright contact channel has not yet been configured. A private channel capable of receiving notices must be published before launch. The browser demo's page-feedback feature stores reports locally and cannot deliver copyright notices to maintainers. Security vulnerability reporting is not a copyright-complaint channel. Rights holders are not required to upload identity documents, contracts or other sensitive evidence to public Issues.

Once the channel is available, notices should identify the affected pages or files, the original work and relationship to its rights, the concern and requested resolution, and a reply contact. Maintainers record receipt and outcomes, verify provenance and permission, restrict display when necessary and contact contributors. Remove or replace material when no applicable permission is confirmed. Handling is not conditional on the claimant first filing a lawsuit, and no unestablished response deadline is promised.
