<a id="about"></a>
## About RocoWiki

RocoWiki is a community encyclopedia for players of the original browser version of Roco Kingdom. It collects pet, skill, battle and adventure information to help players find facts, understand rules and share experience. The site is built by players and is not affiliated with the game publisher.

Website: [wiki.rocoai.app](https://wiki.rocoai.app). The reading site is live; registration, login and formal submissions are not open yet. Some entries remain samples awaiting completion. Check each page's sources and applicable game version.

<a id="reading"></a>
## Find information

- **Pet catalog**: attributes, skills, training and acquisition.
- **Skills and battle mechanics**: effects, bloodlines, type relationships and rules.
- **Events and adventures**: event records, maps, characters and quests.
- **PVP and PVE**: matchup analysis, challenge plans and guides.
- **Tools and search**: calculators and entries searchable by name or keyword.

Check sources, update dates and verification status as you read. Tests and inferences may change with game updates. If you find outdated or incorrect information, evidence helps us improve it.

<a id="contributing"></a>
## Improve the encyclopedia

Submit [content corrections and suggestions](https://github.com/myouo/rocowiki/issues/new/choose) with the affected entry, a reason and verifiable sources. Preserve attribution when quoting others, and keep passwords and private information out of reports.

When formal submissions open, registration and login will be required. Verify your email, then sign in with your email or username and password. Usernames are immutable; display names can change. GitHub binding is optional. Changes are reviewed before publication. Current local points, likes and demo actions are stored on your device and do not represent public community records. See [contributing](CONTRIBUTING.en.md) for content and code contribution requirements.
