<a id="scope"></a>
## Scope

This policy applies to issues, PRs, Wiki submissions, reviews, discussions and communications representing this project. Decisions use conduct and evidence rather than account status, contribution volume, language, gender, age, ethnicity, religion or disability.

<a id="behavior"></a>
## Participation

Respect others and explain your evidence. Critique specific work rather than attacking people. Disagreement, corrections and acknowledging uncertainty are welcome. Harassment, hate speech, threats, exposing private information, sustained personal attacks, impersonation, spam and retaliation against reporters are prohibited. Do not trade points, review privileges or attribution for improper benefits.

<a id="reporting"></a>
## Private conduct reports

The conduct-reporting channel is pending configuration. No maintenance email or dedicated team has been announced. Do not post affected people's information, chat screenshots or private contacts in public issues; request a private contact without details. Vulnerabilities follow the separate [security policy](SECURITY.en.md); its form is not a general conduct-report channel. Before opening community operations, maintainers must publish a working contact and responsible handler.

<a id="handling"></a>
## Handling

A maintainer without a conflict of interest reviews the report, shares information only with people needed for handling, and allows participants to explain. Responses may include a reminder, required correction, participation restrictions or access removal based on severity and recurrence. Retain only necessary records, explain decisions and avoid public disclosure of private information. Another unconflicted maintainer handles review or appeal. No fixed response time or anonymity beyond GitHub's reporting mechanisms is promised.
